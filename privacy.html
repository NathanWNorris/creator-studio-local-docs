Purpose and access

This locally installed, self-hosted Postiz workspace is being evaluated for three accounts owned by the operator. If the owner separately authorizes TikTok access, the proposed evaluation may receive account identifiers, display name, avatar, profile links and biography, public video metadata, and profile/video statistics through the permissions actually granted. The owner reviews the consent screen before access is granted.

Authorization credentials and provider tokens would be kept in the private local application configuration or database on the operator’s PC. The workspace does not ask the owner to give it a TikTok password. The TikTok service handles account sign-in and consent.

Approved video files may be staged locally in the workspace. This evaluation does not authorize TikTok uploads, private or public posts, or scheduling. Native TikTok music selection and durable native TikTok drafts have not been demonstrated by this integration.

The proposed TikTok connection communicates with TikTok to authenticate and request the authorized information. TikTok applies its own terms and privacy policies. This notice does not authorize another relay, hosted phone or public exposure of the local dashboard. No sale of the received TikTok information is planned.

Test configuration and staged files are retained locally while evaluation and owner review continue. Revocation prevents further permitted access; it does not automatically remove every local database, log or protected recovery copy. Any removal must account for preserved project and recovery records.
